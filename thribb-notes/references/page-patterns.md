# Page patterns (observed on viewed lesson pages, restated in original words)

1. Hero band: title, one-line tagline, date, credits, link to the source video if any. Then straight into the seed figure.
2. Exercise block: figure that sets up the question (the question wording is baked into the figure, key term colored), then a prompt to pause, an open notepad box, a Reveal button. Reveal toggles to Hide and opens a grey panel with the worked answer in body text plus a new figure. Hints are separate collapsed rows. A bonus challenge is a collapsed row at the end.
3. Multiple choice: bold question, typeset options, Check disabled until one is chosen. Wrong: button becomes Try Again, a short neutral reaction, no leak. Right: row marked, Reset button, short celebration, worked solution opens. A small character voices feedback.
4. Interactive tool page: canvas full width on a warm band, labelled axes, draggable handles, steppers with a Reset, credit line for the builder.
5. Stepwise figures: each figure adds one idea to the previous one, same mesh/color for "same thing". Cross-sections and cut-aways when a 3D object needs a labelled slice.
6. Principle callout on the figure in the same colors as the pieces it governs.
7. Changing entries only: in a long computation box what moves and arrow it.
8. Prose-only stretches for caveats, history and motivation. Close with a pointer to the next idea or a bonus.
9. Skeptic character: a small character with a speech bubble voices the reader's likely objection right before the example that answers it.
10. Derivations: one equation per line, centered, each introduced by a sentence naming what is being matched. Callout labels with curved arrows on figures for the two ideas being contrasted.
11. Figure that may be a clip: an Image/Video tab pair with a short loop, and a time slider with a live readout when the quantity changes.
