# Script template (two columns)

Write every beat as: SAY | SHOW | WHY THIS BEAT (the move from articulation-playbook.md).

Skeleton for a 6-10 minute explainer:
1. Seed (0:00-0:30): concrete object or puzzle. SHOW the thing. No title card first.
2. Goal and map (0:30-1:00): one sentence of goal, three-step map pinned on screen.
3. Smallest case (1:00-2:30): numbers on screen, a live readout.
4. Naive attempt and its break (2:30-4:00): mark wrong visibly.
5. The idea, built (4:00-6:30): one persistent diagram; colors from the palette table; notation arrives here.
6. Check (6:30-7:30): special case or small run; prediction beat before the reveal.
7. Caveat and scope (7:30-8:00): what was simplified, what was left out.
8. Close (8:00-8:30): one takeaway, one open thread, credits.
