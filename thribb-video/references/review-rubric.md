# Review rubric

Run on the script first, then on the rendered frames, then on the whole.

## The four criteria the creator states (SoME2 results video, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cDofhN-RJqg) (score each: weak, ok, strong; write one sentence of evidence)

1. Motivation, macro: would a skeptical viewer know why this topic matters in the first minute?
2. Motivation, micro: does every definition, step and new object arrive with a reason?
3. Clarity: could the viewer predict the next step one beat early? Are any steps hidden in a jump?
4. Novelty and memorability: is there a fresh angle, a surprising picture or a name that sticks?

## Working advice check (synthesis from the SoME announcement https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ojjzXyQCzso and other reviewed items; not a verbatim list)

- Concrete before abstract? Topic chosen carefully and narrow enough? Genre named and obligations met? Every definition motivated? Audio and legibility checked?

## Known failure modes (opening in abstract philosophy is stated in the SoME2 video; the rest are working synthesis)

- Opening in abstract philosophy. Loud music under explanation. A formula handed down. Add: animation that does not mirror the math; a figure that needs the narration to be decoded.

## Mechanical checks

- Color table respected on every frame.
- Every symbol on screen either defined before use or deferred on purpose with a promise.
- A prediction or pause beat exists at least once in a long video.
- At least one honest caveat or scope statement.
- Credits shown for all outside material.
- Frames inspected at reading size, captioned for silent viewing.
- No copied sentences; no imitation of a person's voice; no paid service used without approval.

## Reporting

State what was checked by looking at pixels, what by reading the script, and what was not checked.
