## Claims in the article that are not verified

- The article describes "Claude Dashboards", "Claude Motion" (a beta on Team and Enterprise plans) and Docs, Slides and Design editing on every plan. Those are product claims about a vendor's current features. They were not checked against the vendor and nothing in this skill depends on them.
- It says a taste skill plus a pipeline skill "gets you 80% of the result". That is the author's opinion with no measurement shown.
- It says no video model touches the frame. That is a description of his setup. It is not a rule this skill adopts.
- The article text contains a leftover draft marker (an "ATTACH: photo" placeholder), so the page may have been published unfinished.
